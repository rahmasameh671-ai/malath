## 🏛️ Brand & Visual Identity

- **Strict Zero Gold Policy:** Strictly zero gold, bronze, brass, or yellow tones across all elements, typography, borders, and partner brand marks.
- **Architectural Corporate Palette:**
  - **Dominant:** Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`), clean alabaster (`#FAFAFA` / `#F8F9FA`), and soft subtle stone/platinum grey (`#EDEDED`).
  - **Accents:** Deep rich charcoal/graphite (`#111827` / `#1A1A1A`) and sophisticated slate blue/graphite for borders and buttons.
  - **Card Containers:** Frosted glassmorphism (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.86)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`) with delicate 1px light grey borders (`#E2E8F0`) and soft ambient drop shadows.
  - **Typography:** Editorial Serif headings (`Cinzel` & `Playfair Display`) paired with clean, ultra-legible Sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) for statistics, body copy, and form elements.
